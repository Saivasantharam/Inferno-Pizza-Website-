Our Story

Born from Fire

A legacy of passion, dough and flame since 1987.

The Beginning

From Naples to Your Neighbourhood

It started with a single wood-fire oven, a grandmother's recipe, and an unshakeable belief that pizza should never be ordinary. In 1987, Salvatore Rossi brought his family's century-old Neapolitan tradition to India, founding Inferno in a modest kitchen in Chennai.

Today, three decades later, every pizza that leaves our oven carries that same soul — hand-stretched dough that has fermented for 48 hours, San Marzano tomatoes imported from Campania, and the roaring heat of a 900°F oak-wood fire.
